Screening & Early Management
Statements 1–15
  • Performance improvement programs & QI strategies STRONG
  • "Code Sepsis" / Sepsis Huddle protocol NEW
  • NEWS / NEWS2 / MEWS / SIRS over qSOFA REVISED
  • Sepsis is clinical — no single biomarker rules it in/out
  • Blood cultures before antimicrobials (if feasible)
  • Blood lactate measurement in shock
  • ≥30 mL/kg IV crystalloid in first 3 hours
  • Early vasopressor if hypotension persists
  • ICU admission within 6 hours if indicated
💊
Infection Management
Statements 16–41
  • Antibiotics within 1 h for septic shock STRONG
  • Antibiotics within 1 h for probable sepsis without shock REVISED
  • Empirical MDR coverage when high-risk factors present
  • Antifungal therapy in high-risk patients
  • Prolonged beta-lactam infusion (maintenance) REVISED
  • Daily de-escalation assessment STRONG
  • Antimicrobial TDM in selected patients
  • Selective digestive decontamination (SDD) in LMV settings
  • Source control — early identification and intervention
❤️
Hemodynamic Management
Statements 42–64
  • Balanced crystalloids over 0.9% normal saline REVISED
  • No starches, no gelatin AGAINST
  • Norepinephrine — first-line vasopressor STRONG
  • Peripheral vasopressors — start early, before central line REVISED
  • MAP target ≥65 mmHg (initial) STRONG
  • MAP 60–65 mmHg for patients ≥65 years NEW
  • Vasopressin added if norepinephrine escalating
  • IV corticosteroids for ongoing vasopressor requirement
  • No terlipressin AGAINST
🫁
Respiratory Support
Statements 65–78
  • SpO₂ 90–95% target for hypoxemic respiratory failure
  • HFNC over conventional O₂ therapy REVISED
  • HFNC as initial therapy over NIPPV
  • Awake prone positioning for non-intubated patients
  • Low tidal volume 6 mL/kg IBW for ARDS STRONG
  • Plateau pressure ≤30 cmH₂O STRONG
  • Prone positioning >12 h/day in moderate-severe ARDS
  • No incremental PEEP titration strategy AGAINST
  • Intermittent NMBA boluses over continuous infusion
  • VV-ECMO when conventional MV fails (experienced centers)
💉
Adjunctive Therapies
Statements 79–99
  • IV corticosteroids in refractory shock
  • No antipyretics for fever alone AGAINST
  • No IV Vitamin C AGAINST
  • No IV immunoglobulins AGAINST
  • No blood purification / hemoperfusion AGAINST
  • No Vitamin D therapy AGAINST
  • Stress ulcer prophylaxis with PPIs (if GI bleed risk)
  • Active fluid removal after resuscitation phase
  • Restrictive transfusion strategy (Hb ≥70 g/L) STRONG
  • Insulin — glucose ≤180 mg/dL STRONG
  • Early enteral nutrition within 72 hours
  • LMWH for VTE prophylaxis STRONG

Goals of Care
Statements 100–107
  • Discuss goals of care with patients & families STRONG
  • Address goals early — within 72 hours
  • Integrate palliative care principles into treatment plan
  • Time-limited trials (TLT) useful when prognosis uncertain
  • Routine formal palliative care consult for all — not recommended AGAINST
  • Shared decision-making with families post-ICU
🔄
Transitions of Care
Statements 108–123
  • Critical care transition programs on ICU transfer NEW
  • Structured handoff at all transitions
  • Pharmacist-led medication reconciliation
  • Screen for social, nutritional, financial support needs
  • Written & verbal sepsis education at discharge NEW
  • Clinician training to support survivors post-hospital
🌱
Long-term Outcomes & Recovery
Statements 124–129
  • Post-ICU follow-up services for all survivors NEW
  • Physical rehabilitation after prolonged MV REVISED
  • Mental health support services after discharge
  • Refer to appropriate professionals for cognitive/psychiatric impairment
  • Health systems facilitate physical, cognitive & emotional assessment
  • Insufficient evidence for cognition-targeted therapies

⚡ Key Shifts from SSC 2021

Screening: NEWS/NEWS2 now favored over qSOFA for in-hospital sepsis screening
Code Sepsis: New conditional recommendation for multidisciplinary code sepsis protocols
Vasopressors: Peripheral initiation now conditionally suggested before central access is secured
MAP Target: New 60–65 mmHg target for elderly patients (≥65 years) added
Beta-lactams: Prolonged infusion now recommended for maintenance (not just bolus)
Vitamin C: IV Vitamin C is now formally recommended AGAINST
Blood Purification: Hemoperfusion, high-dose hemofiltration now recommended AGAINST
PEEP: Incremental PEEP titration now recommended AGAINST in moderate-severe ARDS
Recovery: New domains for transitions of care and long-term post-sepsis outcomes
LMIC: 38% of panelists from LMICs — strongest global equity emphasis to date
